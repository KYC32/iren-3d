Per IREN: Horizon 1 = 75 MW gross (50 MW IT), designed for up to 200 kW racks (Feb 2025 monthly update); 130–200 kW racks and Tier III-equivalent concurrent maintainability (Nov 2025 contract presentation); a packaged chiller plant, chillers, dry coolers and coolant distribution units (CDUs) with UPS and diesel generator back-up (Jan 2025 filing); Horizon 1 is fed from Primary Substation 6 and was delivered to Microsoft on 2026-08-13 (8-K). The hall and yard are drawn from the company's Horizon 1 delivery video (NVL72 rack rows, yellow fibre, copper cold plates, grey electrical lineups along the aisle, big insulated pipes along the buildings, finned-coil coolers). The piping arrangement (cooler → chiller in series), CDU placement and count, transformer shape and temperatures are illustrative; roof and walls are cut away and only part of one rack row is shown. GPU and rack counts and flow rates are estimates from the site's assumptions (2.0 kW per GPU, 72 per rack, a 10 °C water rise). Weather and maintenance timing are compressed.
